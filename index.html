How it runs

  1. Solar first. The array supplies the loads. Any extra charges the batteries.
  2. Batteries fill the gap. At night, on cloudy days or under heavy use, the batteries cover what solar can't.
  3. Generator on low battery. When the battery falls to the start point, the inverter starts the generator over its 2-wire contact.
  4. Generator charges, then stops. The generator carries the loads, puts the rest of its output into the batteries, and shuts off at the stop point.

The generator starts on low battery, not on each load spike. The bottom 20% of the battery stays in reserve, so the usable window is 20–100% (34.4 kWh). Start and stop points are inverter settings; the simulator uses 25% and 90%.

PV strings

Four strings of 9 modules. Phase 1 installs three strings (75%); phase 2 adds the fourth (25%). The split differs from the owner's two-thirds/one-third because string length is fixed by voltage; it front-loads capacity for summer A/C.

MPPTLimits (18kPV)StringsIsc / ImpPhase
  • String voltage: 296V at STC maximum power, inside the 140–500V MPPT range.
  • Cold open-circuit voltage: 407V at the state record low of −37°F (−0.24%/K), under the 550V MPPT protection limit.
  • MPPT 1 clips about 4% at peak irradiance after phase 2.
